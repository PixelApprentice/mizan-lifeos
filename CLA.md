## How to sign

Add this line to the description of your first pull request:

```
I have read the Mizan CLA (CLA.md) and agree to its terms for this and future contributions.
```

The Maintainer records signatures in `.github/cla-signatures.txt`. A CLA bot (for example *cla-assistant*) can automate this. Commits must also carry a `Signed-off-by:` line (`git commit -s`). This is the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/).
