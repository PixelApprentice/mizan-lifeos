Join a team workspace

Ask a workspace admin for an invite (desktop: Team → People → Invite). Scan its QR code or paste the mizan-team:// link. Team data is stored separately from your personal tasks and is never mixed with them.

On the desktop open Settings → Phone companion, enable it, then scan the QR code with this phone's camera. You can also paste the pairing link here.

No Wi-Fi? Use your phone's hotspot or a USB cable

Hotspot: connect the computer to this phone's hotspot. USB: plug in the cable and turn on USB tethering. The app finds the desktop on the new network by itself, or tap Find desktop again. Cable without tethering: turn on USB debugging and press Link phone over USB debugging in the desktop's Settings.

Your tasks, projects and calendar are stored on this phone too. Edits made away from home wait here and merge the next time both devices share a network (newest edit wins per item).
